# AI Provenance Log

Every asset in this project that was generated or assisted by an AI tool gets a row here, added **at the time it is created**. Do not batch this up to do later.

Two reasons this is mandatory:

1. **Steam requires AI content disclosure** at store-page submission. Reconstructing this across ~70 assets months after the fact is miserable.
2. **US copyright requires human authorship.** Purely AI-generated output is not protectable, and prompts alone do not count as authorship — the US Copyright Office treats them as instructions to a machine. What *is* protectable is the human contribution: hand editing, selection, arrangement, composition. The "Human contribution" column below is the record of that, and it's the column that matters most.

Text and code written with AI assistance do not need rows here. Visual and audio assets do.

**Column guidance**

- **Prompt ref** — a pointer into `docs/ART-BIBLE.md`, not the prompt text pasted inline.
- **Human contribution** — be specific and honest. "Aseprite cleanup" is too vague. "Redrew lantern-side shadow ramp, fixed 3 off-palette pixels, hand-animated frames 2–4 of the walk cycle" is the record you want. If the answer is genuinely "none, used as generated," write that — an accurate log is worth more than a flattering one.
- **Notes** — licensing terms of the generating tool, whether commercial use is permitted, anything unusual.

**Also log third-party assets that are not AI-generated** — fonts, sound packs, reference material — with their license in the Notes column. The license you didn't read is a likelier problem than anything else on this page.
